**What is Passive Income?**

Passive income is earnings that require little to no effort to maintain. It's a way to earn money without actively working for it. Examples of passive income include:

1. Dividend-paying stocks
2. Real estate investments
3. Peer-to-peer lending
4. Royalties from creative work
5. Online courses and digital products

**How AI Tools Can Help with Passive Income**

AI tools have revolutionized the way we earn passive income. By automating tasks, analyzing data, and predicting trends, AI-powered tools can help you:

1. **Create and sell online courses**: AI-powered course creation tools can help you design, develop, and deliver online courses with minimal effort.
2. **Generate affiliate income**: AI-driven affiliate marketing tools can help you find and promote high-paying affiliate products.
3. **Create and sell digital products**: AI-powered tools can assist you in designing, developing, and marketing digital products, such as ebooks, software, and plugins.
4. **Invest in stocks**: AI-driven investment tools can help you analyze market trends and make informed investment decisions.
5. **Create and sell ebooks and other digital content**: AI-powered tools can assist you in writing, editing, and publishing digital content.
