**What is Passive Income?**

Before we dive into the world of AI-powered passive income, let's define what passive income is. Passive income refers to earnings that are generated without actively trading time for money. This can include:

1. **Investments**: Dividends, interest, or rental income from investments.
2. **Online businesses**: Creating and selling digital products, such as ebooks, courses, or software.
3. **Royalties**: Income generated from creative works, such as books, music, or art.
4. **Affiliate marketing**: Earning commissions by promoting products or services.
5. **Real estate**: Renting out properties or investing in real estate investment trusts (REITs).

**How AI Tools Can Help with Passive Income**

Artificial intelligence (AI) has revolutionized the way we live and work. From virtual assistants to predictive analytics, AI tools are designed to automate tasks, streamline processes, and provide insights. In the context of passive income, AI tools can help with:

1. **Automating tasks**: AI-powered tools can automate routine tasks, such as data entry, bookkeeping, or customer service.
2. **Predictive analytics**: AI tools can analyze data to predict trends, identify opportunities, and make informed decisions.
3. **Content creation**: AI-powered tools can generate content, such as articles, social media posts, or even entire books.
4. **Marketing and advertising**: AI tools can optimize marketing campaigns, personalize advertising, and improve conversion rates.
