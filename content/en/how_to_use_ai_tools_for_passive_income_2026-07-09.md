**What is Passive Income?**

Passive income refers to earnings that require little to no effort to maintain or generate. It's the opposite of active income, which requires you to actively work for every dollar you earn. Passive income can take many forms, including:

1. Dividends from stocks
2. Rent from property
3. Royalties from creative work
4. Interest from savings accounts
5. Sales from online products or services

**How AI Tools Can Help**

AI tools have revolutionized the way we live and work. They can perform tasks quickly and efficiently, freeing up time for more important things. When it comes to passive income, AI tools can help in several ways:

1. **Content creation**: AI-powered tools can generate high-quality content, such as articles, videos, and social media posts, saving you time and effort.
2. **Marketing automation**: AI tools can help automate marketing tasks, such as email marketing, social media management, and lead generation.
3. **Financial analysis**: AI-powered tools can analyze financial data, providing insights and recommendations to help you make informed investment decisions.
4. **E-commerce**: AI tools can help manage online stores, including product recommendations, inventory management, and customer service.
