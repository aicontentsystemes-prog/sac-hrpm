## **1. What is Passive Income?**
Passive income refers to earnings that require little to no ongoing effort to maintain. Unlike active income (like a salary), passive income continues generating revenue even when you’re not working.

Examples of passive income include:
- Rental income
- Dividends from investments
- Royalties from books or music
- Affiliate marketing
- Digital product sales

With AI, you can automate many of these income streams.

---

## **2. Why Use AI for Passive Income?**
AI tools can:
✅ **Automate repetitive tasks** (e.g., content creation, customer service)
✅ **Generate high-quality content** (blogs, eBooks, social media posts)
✅ **Optimize marketing campaigns** (AI-driven ads, email automation)
✅ **Trade stocks & crypto** (AI algos for trading)
✅ **Create and sell digital products** (AI-generated art, courses)

AI reduces the time and effort needed, making passive income more achievable.
