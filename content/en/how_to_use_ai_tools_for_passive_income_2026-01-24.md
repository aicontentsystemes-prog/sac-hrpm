**What is Passive Income?**

Before we dive into the AI tools, let's define passive income. Passive income is earned from investments or activities that require little to no direct effort to maintain. It's the opposite of active income, which requires regular input and effort to generate. Examples of passive income include dividend payments, rent from real estate investments, and royalties from creative works.
