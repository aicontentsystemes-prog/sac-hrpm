**What is Passive Income?**

Passive income is earnings that require little to no effort to maintain or generate. It's the opposite of active income, which requires a person's direct involvement and time commitment. Examples of passive income include:

* Renting out a property on Airbnb or renting out a timeshare
* Investing in dividend-paying stocks or real estate investment trusts (REITs)
* Creating and selling online courses or ebooks
* Affiliate marketing
* Creating a mobile app or a game
