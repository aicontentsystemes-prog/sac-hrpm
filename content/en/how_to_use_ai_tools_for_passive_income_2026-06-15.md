**What are AI Tools for Passive Income?**

AI tools for passive income are software applications that utilize machine learning algorithms and natural language processing (NLP) to automate tasks, generate content, and even invest in the stock market. These tools can help you create digital products, such as ebooks, courses, and software, without requiring extensive expertise or manual labor. Before we dive into the practical steps, let's explore the benefits of using AI tools for passive income:
