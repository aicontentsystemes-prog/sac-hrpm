**What is Passive Income?**

Passive income is earnings that require little to no effort to maintain. It's a way to generate income without being actively involved in the process. Examples of passive income include:

1. **Dividend-paying stocks**: Receive regular payments from your investments without having to actively manage them.
2. **Renting out properties**: Earn rental income from properties you own, without having to constantly manage them.
3. **Creating and selling online courses**: Once you've created a course, you can sell it multiple times without having to invest additional time.
4. **Affiliate marketing**: Earn commissions from promoting products or services without having to create them.

**How AI Tools Can Help with Passive Income**

AI (Artificial Intelligence) tools can help you create and maintain passive income streams in several ways:

1. **Automating tasks**: AI can automate repetitive tasks, freeing up your time to focus on higher-level tasks.
2. **Analyzing data**: AI can analyze complex data sets, providing insights that can inform your investment decisions.
3. **Predictive modeling**: AI can build predictive models that forecast market trends, helping you make informed decisions.
4. **Content creation**: AI can generate high-quality content, such as blog posts, social media posts, and even entire books.
