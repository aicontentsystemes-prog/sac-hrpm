## **1. What Is Passive Income?**
Passive income refers to earnings generated with little to no daily effort. Unlike traditional jobs, passive income streams continue to generate money even when you're not actively working.

Examples of passive income include:
- **Affiliate marketing**
- **Digital product sales (eBooks, courses, templates)**
- **Print-on-demand businesses**
- **YouTube ad revenue**
- **Automated blogging and content creation**

AI can automate many of these processes, making it easier to scale and sustain passive income.

---

## **2. Why Use AI for Passive Income?**
AI tools can:
✅ **Automate repetitive tasks** (content creation, social media posting)
✅ **Reduce costs** (no need to hire freelancers for basic tasks)
✅ **Increase efficiency** (generate high-quality outputs faster)
✅ **Scale revenue streams** (expand without extra effort)

By leveraging AI, you can focus on strategy while the tools handle execution.

### **C. AI Video Creation Tools**

### **D. AI Affiliate & E-Commerce Tools**
