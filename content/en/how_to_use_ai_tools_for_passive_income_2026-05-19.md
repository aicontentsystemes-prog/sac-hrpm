**What is Passive Income?**

Before we dive into the world of AI tools, let's define passive income. Passive income refers to earnings that require little to no direct effort or time from you to generate. This can include:

1. Renting out properties
2. Investing in dividend-paying stocks
3. Creating and selling digital products
4. Licensing intellectual property
5. Generating passive income through AI-driven tools

**Why Use AI Tools for Passive Income?**

AI tools offer a game-changing opportunity for passive income, providing:

1. **Scalability**: AI tools can process vast amounts of data and automate tasks, allowing you to scale your income without sacrificing time or effort.

3. **Diversification**: AI tools can help you diversify your income streams, reducing reliance on a single source of income.
