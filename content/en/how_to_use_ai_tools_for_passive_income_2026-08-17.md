**What is Passive Income?**

Before we dive into the world of AI tools, let's define what passive income is. Passive income refers to earnings that require little to no effort to maintain or generate. It's the opposite of active income, which demands your direct involvement. Examples of passive income include:

1. **Dividend-paying stocks**: Companies distribute a portion of their profits to shareholders in the form of dividends.
2. **Real estate investment trusts (REITs)**: Investors can earn rental income without directly managing properties.
3. **Peer-to-peer lending**: Individuals lend money to others, earning interest without direct involvement.
4. **Royalties**: Creators earn money from their original work, such as music, art, or literature.
