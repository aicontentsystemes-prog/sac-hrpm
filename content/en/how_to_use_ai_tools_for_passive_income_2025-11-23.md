**What is AI-Powered Passive Income?**

Before we dive into the nitty-gritty, let's define what we mean by AI-powered passive income. Essentially, it refers to earning money without actively working for it, using AI-driven tools and platforms to generate revenue. This can include a wide range of activities, such as:

1. Creating and selling digital products (e.g., ebooks, courses, software)
2. Investing in AI-driven assets (e.g., cryptocurrencies, stocks, real estate)
3. Participating in online marketplaces and gig economy platforms
4. Building and monetizing online communities and forums
5. Developing and licensing AI-powered apps and software
