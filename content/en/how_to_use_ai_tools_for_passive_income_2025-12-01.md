**What is Passive Income?**

Before we dive into the world of AI tools, let's define what passive income is. Passive income refers to earnings that require little to no effort to maintain or generate. It's a type of income that can be earned through various means, such as investments, real estate, and online businesses.

**How Can AI Tools Generate Passive Income?**

AI tools can generate passive income in several ways:

1. **Content creation**: AI-powered content creation tools can generate high-quality content, such as articles, videos, and social media posts, that can be monetized through advertising, sponsorships, or affiliate marketing.
2. **E-commerce**: AI-powered e-commerce tools can help you automate and optimize your online store, generating passive income through sales and commissions.
3. **Investing**: AI-powered investment tools can help you make informed investment decisions, generating passive income through dividend-paying stocks, real estate investment trusts (REITs), or peer-to-peer lending.
4. **Affiliate marketing**: AI-powered affiliate marketing tools can help you optimize your affiliate marketing campaigns, generating passive income through commissions.
