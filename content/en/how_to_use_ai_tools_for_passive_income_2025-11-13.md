**What is Passive Income?**

Passive income is earnings that require little to no effort to maintain or increase. It's a way to earn money while you sleep, travel, or focus on other important aspects of your life. Passive income can come in various forms, such as:

1. **Dividend-paying stocks**: Receiving regular dividends from stocks you've invested in.
2. **Renting out a property**: Generating income from renting out a property on Airbnb or a long-term rental agreement.
3. **Creating and selling digital products**: Selling digital products, such as ebooks, courses, or software, that can be sold multiple times with minimal effort.
4. **Affiliate marketing**: Earning commissions by promoting products or services of other companies.

**How AI Tools Can Help with Passive Income**

AI tools have revolutionized the way we work and live. They can automate tasks, analyze data, and make predictions, making it possible to create a passive income stream with minimal effort. Here are some ways AI tools can help:

1. **Content creation**: AI-powered content generation tools can create high-quality content, such as articles, videos, and social media posts, that can attract and engage your audience.
2. **Email marketing**: AI-powered email marketing tools can automate email campaigns, personalize messages, and optimize subject lines to increase open rates and conversions.
3. **Social media management**: AI-powered social media management tools can schedule posts, analyze engagement, and provide insights to optimize your social media strategy.
4. **Financial analysis**: AI-powered financial analysis tools can analyze financial data, provide predictions, and offer investment advice to help you make informed decisions.
